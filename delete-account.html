Delete your RidePulse account

RidePulse, by Lungelo Melusi Dlamini

Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your account does not stop Google Play or Apple from billing you. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or in your Apple ID subscription settings.

In the app

  1. Open RidePulse and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email lungelomelusid@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my RidePulse account", from the email address linked to your Uber account. We'll confirm it's you, delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email, Uber driver ID, city and notification settings
  • Your logged shifts and AI earnings insights
  • Any community pins and safety reports you posted
  • Your referral code, referral history and bonus days
  • Your notification history and push notification token

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat, which they keep under their own policies and tax law.
  • Drivers you referred keep their own accounts and bonus days. Only the link to you is removed.
