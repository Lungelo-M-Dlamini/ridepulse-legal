RidePulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: RidePulse uses your Uber login only to confirm you're a driver. We keep your name, email, city and app settings, plus any shifts you choose to log. Your location is used only on your phone to pick your city, and to place a safety report when you post one. We never see your card details and we don't sell your data or show ads. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers. We share information only with these service providers, and only what each needs to do its job:

  • Supabase hosts our database and servers, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Uber confirms your driver status when you sign in.
  • Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat process subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your RidePulse account ID and your purchase records.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push notifications using your notification token.
  • Mapbox provides the map. Your phone requests map images from Mapbox, which sees your IP address and the map area you're viewing. When you search for a place, the text you type and the city you're viewing are sent to Mapbox to find matches; we don't store your searches. We have turned off Mapbox's optional usage tracking.
  • Anthropic writes your AI earnings insight (Elite plan). When you ask for one, we send a summary of your logged shifts (totals by day, hour and zone), not your name or email.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Information sent outside South Africa

Our database is in the European Union, and some service providers above process information in the United States. We only use providers that are bound by agreements or laws giving your information protection comparable to POPIA, as POPIA section 72 requires.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account information, settings, logged shifts and referral records: until you delete your account.
  • Busy-area pins: deleted automatically after 45 minutes.
  • Safety reports and confirmations: shown for up to 6 hours, deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Posts you report for moderation (and a copy of the note you reported): kept for 90 days so we can review them, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: Google, Apple and RevenueCat keep these under their own policies and tax law.

6. How we protect it

All information is encrypted in transit. Our database only lets each driver read their own personal information, and only verified drivers can see shared map data. Access to the systems is limited to people who need it to run RidePulse.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your account and its information (see below);
  • object to how we process your information;
  • complain to the Information Regulator.

Email lungelomelusid@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, settings, pins, reports, logged shifts and referral records. If you can't access the app, email us and we will delete it for you. Full steps are on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or your Apple ID settings, or you'll keep being charged.

8. Children

RidePulse is for registered drivers and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
